__IDENTIFIER_NOTE__

The SEC investment schedule includes securities-lending collateral. N-PORT includes cash collateral and cash-management positions, not just equities. Securities-lending collateral has an offsetting obligation elsewhere, so investment weights can exceed 100% and the as-filed list can differ from the issuer's published top ten. The default view includes it; the optional view excludes only affirmatively flagged cash collateral. We never renormalize weights.

Sources, provenance and limitations

Holdings come from public SEC N-PORT factual fields. Display rounding does not change source weights or ranking. No monthly records are invented between quarterly reports.

Only normalized factual holdings and metadata are archived, not full filing XML, narrative notes or signatures. SEC publication is not a blanket public-domain designation or a third-party data license. The source links identify the original filings; this project does not license their prose or other third-party materials.

Amendments supersede their original reported period by acceptance time, then accession. Prior normalized versions and older reported quarters remain archived. Additional public reporting months are not counted as quarterly portfolios.

Weights are percentages of fund net assets, not of the selected ten. Securities-lending cash collateral is identified by the filing's affirmative collateral flag and cross-checked against STIV/RF asset and issuer categories. Other cash-management vehicles are not silently called collateral. The optional operating-companies view excludes only that affirmative flag, then reranks the entire remaining portfolio by unrounded weights. It is not an equity-only classification: other cash vehicles and derivatives remain eligible. No omitted weight is redistributed to the remaining positions. The investment schedule need not sum to 100% because of cash, liabilities or derivatives. Zero/negative values and unusual reported totals are retained, not normalized. Holdings changes do not measure purchases or sales: prices, fund flows and corporate actions also affect weights.

TradingView retains its widget branding and attribution. Its hosted chart is not a downloadable data feed or part of the SEC archive. No prices are scraped, stored or included in the holdings CSVs. Historical holdings are not investment advice.
