Sources and methodology

Every response is checked against its requested exchange date and fund ID. All full-portfolio weight totals are checked before selecting the top ten. Cash and other asset classes are eligible for ranking. Displayed percentages are rounded to two decimals; exports retain unrounded numeric weights. No full-list allocation discrepancy is normalized away.

Source: . Each selected month links to its dated source. The three relative CSV downloads contain all 60 months regardless of the filters above, and work without JavaScript.

Historical portfolio data is not investment advice and does not measure purchases or sales: weights also change with prices, fund flows and corporate actions. Unfinished months are excluded.
